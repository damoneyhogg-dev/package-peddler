# Asset license register

No external art, character, animation, vehicle, sound, or environment asset has been added yet.

## Approved character-source plan (not yet imported)

- MPFB / core MakeHuman assets: CC0. Official license: <https://static.makehumancommunity.org/about/license.html>
- MakeHuman commercial-use FAQ: <https://static.makehumancommunity.org/makehuman/faq/can_i_sell_models_created_with_makehuman.html>
- Adobe Mixamo animations: royalty-free use in commercial video games under Adobe's Mixamo FAQ: <https://helpx.adobe.com/creative-cloud/faq/mixamo-faq.html>
- Community-added MakeHuman clothing, hair, or other assets are not approved automatically; each requires a separate license record before use.
- Keep dated copies of the applicable license pages when the assets are acquired.

Before an asset enters the project, record its title, creator, source URL, acquisition date, license text/link, allowed commercial distribution, modification rights, attribution requirement, and original archive hash.

## Approved pipeline sources (no asset imported yet)

- MakeHuman bundled assets/output: CC0 per the official `LICENSE.md`; third-party community assets are excluded until separately reviewed.
- Mixamo characters/animations: free with Adobe ID and royalty-free for video games per Adobe's official Mixamo FAQ; do not redistribute source assets as an asset library.
- Owner-supplied videos: private likeness reference only; extracted frames are excluded from Git and release packages.
