# First playable vertical slice

The slice is complete only when one continuous saved game can:

1. Start at home.
2. Walk to a vehicle.
3. Enter and drive the vehicle.
4. Travel to a warehouse.
5. Park, exit, and enter the warehouse.
6. Compare and accept one route.
7. Scan and load its packages.
8. Drive to five fictional delivery locations.
9. Retrieve the correct package at each stop.
10. Scan, place, and confirm each delivery.
11. Resolve at least one believable complication.
12. Finish or return the route.
13. Receive gross, expense, and net earnings.
14. Save, close, reload, and retain progress.

No static page, menu-only simulation, teleport-only route, or noninteractive cinematic satisfies this milestone.

## Development order

1. Character movement and animation integration.
2. Camera and controller systems.
3. Vehicle entry, exit, and driving.
4. Warehouse, route selection, scanning, and loading.
5. Five-stop delivery loop, GPS, earnings, and persistence.
